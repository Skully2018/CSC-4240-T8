How recommendations work

RMR compares recipe ingredients with the household’s taste preferences and uses those matches to order recipe suggestions.

Taste and restrictions

Ingredient preferences help identify meals that suit the people at the table. A matched neutral ingredient adds modest fit, while a neutral ingredient absent from the recipe does not lower the score. Likes and strong likes count more.

Allergies, diet rules, and ingredients in “Always exclude” are checked before recipe ranking. Those restrictions can remove a recipe even when its other ingredients match the household’s tastes. A roommate with no matching preferences for a recipe is shown as “Not scored”; their missing match lowers household happiness, and recipes matching more roommates rank first.

Scores and feedback

Colored percentages summarize how well a recipe fits the listed household preferences. They are estimates based on the recipe information provided by TheMealDB, so check ingredients and product labels before cooking.

Household ratings on recipes already tried can also help order future results. Ratings are saved separately from bookmarks, and some unrated recipes remain in the results so the household can discover new meals.

Score colors: red is a low fit, yellow is a partial fit, and green is a strong fit.
