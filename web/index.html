Set up Gemini’s free-tier API
  1. Create your own key at Google AI Studio.
  2. Use a Google project on the Free tier, without billing enabled. Free availability and quotas depend on Google and your account.
  3. Copy .env.example to .env beside main.py and set GEMINI_API_KEY. Keep this file private.
  4. Click Check setup, then enter your description above. The setup check does not send a model request.

No model download, GPU, or Ollama installation is needed. Google may use free-tier content to improve its products under its terms. The app cannot verify your project’s billing tier; check it in AI Studio. See README.md for full setup instructions.
